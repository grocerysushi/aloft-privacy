Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aloft ("Aloft," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Aloft mobile application (the "App"). Aloft is operated by Kyle Shaft. If you have questions, contact us at hello@thethurible.com.

Summary

Aloft is a flight tracking app. We do not require an account, we do not ask for your name, and we do not build a profile about you. The App uses your device location only to find aircraft and weather near you, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Information We Collect

Precise location. When you grant location permission, the App accesses your device's precise location. Your location is used to determine which aircraft and weather data to request and display for your area. We use location for app functionality only.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or any account information, because Aloft has no account system.

How Your Location Is Used

To show nearby aircraft and weather, the App sends location based requests to third party data providers. These requests are made to fetch the data you see in the App. The providers we use are:

  • Airplanes.Live and ADSB.fi for live aircraft position and ADS-B data
  • adsbdb for aircraft, route, and registration lookups
  • RainViewer for weather radar imagery
  • CARTO for map tiles and basemap rendering

Your location may be included in these requests so the providers can return data relevant to your area. Each provider processes data under its own privacy practices. We do not control and are not responsible for the data practices of these third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not link the data we process to your identity, because we do not collect identifying information.

Subscriptions and Payments

Aloft offers an auto renewing subscription, Aloft Flight Pro, including an introductory free trial. All billing is handled by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of your purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

Children's Privacy

Aloft is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

Aloft does not maintain user accounts or a backend database of your activity. Location is used in real time to make data requests and is not retained by us for profiling.

Your Choices

You can disable location access at any time in your device Settings. If you disable location, the App's nearby features will not function, but you can continue to use other parts of the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@thethurible.com.
