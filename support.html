Common questions

Some planes aren't showing. Aloft uses community ADS-B data, so coverage depends on nearby receivers; military and privacy-blocked aircraft may not appear.

The map is empty. Make sure Location is allowed in Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Aloft Flight, and that you have a connection.

Managing your subscription. Open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions to view, change, or cancel Aloft Flight Pro.
