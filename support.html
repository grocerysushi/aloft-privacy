Common questions

Some planes aren't showing. Aloft uses community ADS-B data, so coverage depends on nearby receivers; military and privacy-blocked aircraft may not appear.

The map is empty. Make sure Location is allowed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Aloft Flight, and that you have a connection.

Re-downloading the app. Aloft Flight is a one-time purchase. If you reinstall it or get a new device, download it again from the App Store while signed in with the same Apple Account — there's no extra charge. (App Store → your profile → Purchased.)
